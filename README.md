# IV Surface Sampling & Activity Clocks — Replication Package

Replication materials for the article
《对齐目标误差最低 0.45 倍，用错设计最高放大 2.03 倍：IV 曲面抽样协议的合成复测》
("Error down to 0.45x when the design matches the target, up to 2.03x when it
does not: a ground-truth audit of IV surface sampling"), published on
[vxi.me](https://vxi.me).

The article audits the survey-sampling and activity-clock claims of:

> Bastola & Prushansky, *Compiling and forecasting implied volatility surfaces
> via survey sampling, tempered inverse-probability sequence learning, and
> causal activity clocks*, arXiv:2610.33438 (v1, 2026-10-24).

The paper ships no code, so the protocol printed in its §3.1–3.2 and §5 was
reimplemented clean-room and run on **synthetic populations with known ground
truth**, under a protocol frozen before any design comparison (`PROTOCOL.md`).
All data in this package is synthetic and generated by the included code;
nothing here is market data.

## What was run

**Arm A — IV surface compilation** (`code/surface-sampling/arm_a.py`).
A synthetic NVDA-like option-chain population (200 days, 111,573 contracts,
parametric ground-truth surface) is sub-sampled at retention fractions
α ∈ {0.25, 0.40, 0.60, 0.80} by four designs (SRS, Neyman allocation,
equal-cell allocation, liquidity PPS with Madow systematic sampling) and
scored against equal-weighted (EW) and liquidity-weighted (LW) targets.

- EW side **reproduced**: equal-cell ratios vs SRS 0.73/0.68/0.59/0.45,
  Neyman 0.82/0.83/0.78/0.72; plug-in coverage 100%.
- PPS misapplied to EW inflates error **1.66–2.03×** (paper: 2.47–3.90×).
- **Not reproduced as stated**: on its own matched LW target, PPS (0.81/0.74/
  0.61/0.50) is beaten at every α by equal-cell allocation (0.72/0.67/0.57/
  0.42). The paper's "by construction" dominance is a property of its
  corpus's joint distribution, not a construction guarantee. Mechanism:
  per-cell sample starvation (`results/surface-sampling/percell.csv`,
  `percell_decomposition.txt`).
- Robustness (`arm_a_constnoise.py`): with observation noise made
  spread-independent, the LW ordering persists (EqCell 0.35–0.52 vs PPS
  0.51–0.86), so the noise–weight coupling does not drive the result.

**Arm B — activity clocks** (`code/activity-clocks/arm_b_v2.py`).
Synthetic intraday process (320 sessions × 390 minutes, GARCH(1,1)
clustering, U-shaped seasonality, 8.6× price expansion). Rolling 60-session
volume/dollar clocks hold ~9.4/11.3 bars per session; pooled interior excess
kurtosis 0.69/0.55 vs 8.73 for time bars; clustering is not removed and
next-bar direction accuracy stays at 0.493–0.510 — direction reproduced.
The first population version was discarded before any verdict for validity
failures (kept as `arm_b.py` + `results_v1_invalid.json`); the minimal fix
and its reasons are logged in `PROTOCOL.md`.

**Not run**: the paper's real OptionsDX NVDA corpus arm (the corpus sits
behind the vendor's account/checkout flow; no account was created), and the
paper's longitudinal / forecasting sections, which the article reports as
author-reported only. See `run-receipt.md` and `claim-status-table.md`.

## Layout

- `PROTOCOL.md` — the frozen pre-registered protocol, deviations D1–D7
- `run-receipt.md` — full test receipt with verdicts and review addenda
- `claim-status-table.md` — every article claim mapped to its evidence status
- `code/surface-sampling/` — Arm A runner, constant-noise robustness variant,
  per-cell diagnostic
- `code/activity-clocks/` — Arm B v2 runner (and the discarded v1)
- `results/` — the exact JSON/CSV outputs the article's numbers come from

## Running

Python 3 with NumPy (developed on numpy 1.26.4 / scipy 1.11.4 / pandas
2.1.4). No downloads, no accounts, no API keys.

```bash
python3 code/surface-sampling/arm_a.py            # writes results.json next to the script's copy
python3 code/surface-sampling/arm_a_constnoise.py # robustness variant
python3 code/surface-sampling/percell_diag.py     # writes percell.csv
python3 code/activity-clocks/arm_b_v2.py          # Arm B v2
```

Seeds are fixed in the scripts (Arm A: 20261103; Arm B: 20261104). Ratio
tables reproduce exactly; Monte Carlo diagnostics (per-cell RMSEs) vary by a
few bps across RNG streams.

## Boundaries

Single run per arm, our parameterization, our synthetic populations. The
verdicts bound the paper's claims as *construction* claims; they neither
confirm nor refute the paper's numbers on its own NVDA panels. This package
is a methods audit, not investment advice.
